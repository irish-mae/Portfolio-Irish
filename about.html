THE SHORT VERSION

I work across technology, project coordination, operations, and community engagement.

Across academic, professional, and community settings, my work has often required more than a good idea. It has meant understanding the objective, organizing requirements and resources, coordinating the people involved, and helping the work get done.

That is the thread connecting my experience—from an LLM-augmented academic policy chatbot to community initiatives, student leadership, event delivery, and data operations.
